# Game Concept Brainstorm

18 original concepts explored before selecting **Understudy Kingdom**
(#17). Kept as a reference record — some may be worth revisiting for a
future title or as a feature idea within Understudy Kingdom.

## Round 1

1. **Sediment** — reverse-archaeology puzzler. Bury artifacts correctly so a
   future AI archaeologist draws the right conclusions about a civilization
   you're inventing.
2. **Sublet** — landlord social-deduction sim. Manage one apartment building
   of AI tenants with needs/secrets/grudges; other players' tenants sublet
   into your building carrying reputations earned elsewhere.
3. **Undertow** — one-thumb swimming game about surrendering control, not
   precision input; levels are ocean-current "compositions."
4. **Franchise Row** — inverted tycoon game. Run one single fast-food
   location forever; success is community reputation, not empire growth.
5. **Molt** — growth-through-loss idle/roguelite. Leveling up sheds an
   ability; meta-progression is a "memory garden" of shed abilities to graft
   onto future runs.
6. **Quiet Hours** — async cozy sim where actions only resolve while your
   phone is asleep; explicitly anti-engagement-bait.

## Round 2

7. **Overheard** — eavesdropping mystery via spatial audio; mysteries unfold
   by physically positioning near NPCs without being noticed.
8. **Compost** — a sim where deliberate decay (letting things rot) is the
   resource that fuels growth; inverts "keep everything pristine."
9. **Ledger** — found-footage accounting thriller; gameplay is spreadsheet
   reconciliation, story is a slow-burn conspiracy.
10. **Understudy** — theater troupe game; you're always the backup actor,
    rehearsing every role, going on stage only when the lead flakes.
11. **Ballast** — physics puzzle where adding weight/drag is often the
    winning move, inverting typical "get lighter/faster" puzzle logic.
12. **Neighbor Watch** — passive-aggressive HOA sim played entirely through
    anonymous notes; multiplayer version mixes real players' notes in.
13. **Tide Pool** — creature-collector where creatures only exist for one
    ~10-15 min tide cycle; every choice is made knowing it's temporary.

## Round 3 — market-informed (post competitor research)

14. **Drawdown** — merge game that runs backward: start maxed-out, spend
    inventory down to zero by a deadline. Reuses proven merge mechanics,
    inverts the psychological hook from hoarding to relief.
15. **Shift Lead** — match-3 reskinned as staffing logistics (restaurant/
    warehouse rush) instead of candy/gems — same mechanic, grounded meta-layer.
16. **Slow Boil** — idle/fermentation sim where rushing ruins the batch;
    only major idle mechanic that punishes the tap-tap-tap impulse.
17. **Understudy Kingdom** — strategy/RPG hybrid where the player advises a
    flawed NPC ruler instead of commanding one. **Selected.** See
    `PROJECT_PLAN.md` for full spec.
18. **Held Breath** — puzzle genre where the core resource is restraint
    (not making a move) instead of speed; waiting reveals more of the board.

## Why Understudy Kingdom

- Sits in the single largest mobile revenue combo (Strategy $17.5B/yr + RPG
  $16.8B/yr), unlike the more experimental picks (#7, #9, #11) which have no
  proven revenue comp.
- Reuses base-building/army-strategy mechanics the market already knows
  convert, keeping technical risk lower than a fully novel mechanic like
  #16 (Slow Boil) or #8 (Compost).
- The differentiation is framing + trust (advisor-not-ruler, no
  unwinnable-without-spending events, async-only PvP) rather than an
  unproven core loop — see `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md` for the specific
  complaints this counters.
- Runner-up for a lower-cost solo/small-team prototype: **Drawdown (#14)** —
  cheapest to build since it's a straight mechanical inversion of an
  already-proven merge loop.
